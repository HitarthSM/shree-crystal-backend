## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Shree Crystal Co-op Credit and Consumers Society Limited, registered under the Gujarat Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, registration number [TODO: Insert Registration Number], collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of its members through this website and member portal.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Identity and contact details**: full name, father's/husband's name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile number, email
- **KYC documents**: Aadhaar number, PAN, photograph
- **Membership details**: membership date, share capital, nominee name and relation
- **Financial records**: account statements, loan details (where applicable)
- **Technical data**: login activity and IP address, for security purposes only

## 3. Why we collect it
We collect this information to:
- Maintain your membership and statutory records as required under our society's bye-laws and the Gujarat Co-operative Societies Act, 1961
- Deliver account statements and notices to you
- Calculate loan/deposit interest where you choose to use the calculator
- Respond to queries and maintain records for our annual statutory audit
- Detect and prevent unauthorized access to your account

## 4. How we use and share your information
We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:
- With our statutory auditor and the Registrar of Cooperative Societies, when legally required
- With our SMS/email service provider, solely to deliver OTPs and notices to you, under a confidentiality obligation
- When required by law, court order, or a competent government authority

## 5. How we protect your information
Aadhaar and PAN numbers are stored encrypted and shown to you and our staff only in masked form. Access to member records is restricted to authorized staff on a need-to-know basis, and every access or change to your record is logged.

## 6. Your rights
You may:
- View your own profile and statements at any time by logging in
- Request a correction to your personal details through the "Request Edit" option, which is reviewed by our staff
- Contact our Grievance Officer (Section below) with any question about how your data is used

## 7. Data retention
We retain your membership and financial records for as long as your membership is active, and thereafter for the period required under our bye-laws and applicable cooperative society regulations, even after account closure.

## 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
